# CAATH Client Accounting Add-on

## Product decision

CAATH will offer client accounting as an optional, separately priced workspace for each client whose books are maintained by a professional firm or practice. The practice's own CAATH billing and practice accounting remain separate. This avoids charging every customer for a heavy module they may not use and preserves clear client-level data ownership.

## Commercial model

- Client Books pilot suggestion: ₹499 per client workspace per month.
- Client Books Pro pilot suggestion: ₹999 per client workspace per month.
- God Admin sets and approves the actual price before activation.
- No request can self-activate or create a charge.
- Revisit pricing after the pilot based on transaction volume, storage, support load and GST/bank automation costs.

Zoho Books currently starts its paid Standard tier at ₹899 per organisation per month, or ₹749 when billed annually. CAATH can enter below that level during the pilot because the early module is designed for professional-practice-operated client books rather than full standalone business accounting.

## Delivery sequence

1. **Foundation — implemented:** per-client add-on requests, tenant isolation, God Admin pricing/activation and responsive workspace entry.
2. **Core books:** chart of accounts, opening balances, balanced journal/payment/receipt/purchase/sales vouchers, period locking and immutable audit trail.
3. **Reports:** ledger, day book, trial balance, profit and loss, balance sheet, receivables and payables.
4. **India compliance:** GST tax ledgers, HSN/SAC, e-invoice/e-way bill readiness, TDS and GST reconciliation.
5. **Automation:** bank-statement import and matching, Tally/Excel import, recurring entries, document-to-voucher assistance and client approvals.

CAATH should not advertise Tally or Zoho Books parity until phases 2–4 pass accounting, tax, migration, security and reconciliation testing.

## Template Studio

The first release includes original CAATH Word working drafts for Directors' Report, Board Meeting Minutes, AGM Notice and Management Representation Letter. It follows the useful interaction pattern seen in public template tools—enter core details, generate, download—but does not reproduce third-party template text. Each output is editable and clearly requires professional review before signing or filing.
